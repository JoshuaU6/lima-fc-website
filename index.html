Welcome to Lima FC

Lima Football Club is a community of players, coaches and supporters who share one thing: a love for the game and for each other. Whether you’re lacing up for training on a weekday evening or cheering from the stands on matchday, you belong here.

We train together every Thursday evening, run our own end-of-year tournament, and keep the club honest, open and well-run through an elected executive committee. Every member has a voice, and every member’s dues go straight back into the club.

If you’re new, come down on a Thursday between 5 and 7 pm, say hello to the committee, and register when you’re ready. We’d love to have you.
